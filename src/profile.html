  • Time, Duration and Money built in.

overdue.vibe
# Invoices more than 30 days past due
def overdue(invoices: array) -> array
  cutoff = 30.days.ago(Time.now)

  invoices.select do |inv|
    Time.parse(inv[:due_on]) < cutoff
  end
end

What we believe

Five rules decide what we build and what we cut.

  1. 1.Individuals first. One person should be able to ship a good idea without hiring a team or renting a platform.
  2. 2.Local first. Keep your data on your machine. Use the cloud when it helps. Don't live there.
  3. 3.AI is a collaborator. Use it to make more, better, stranger things. Don't use it to make people optional.
  4. 4.We make money selling useful software and services. Your data and attention aren’t for sale.
  5. 5.Small, fast, finished. A tool should open instantly, do its job, and get out of your way.
