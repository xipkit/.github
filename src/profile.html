What we believe

Why we do what we do

Manifesto

We believe people want to make things.

To explore an idea. To solve a problem. To build something that didn’t exist before.

AI gives us a new kind of tool, one that can help us think, create, and build in ways that weren’t possible before.

We want that power to belong to the person using it.

So we build tools that are small, fast, and personal. They run on your computer. You own them. You can understand them, change them, and make them yours.

The best tools don’t replace the person using them. They make the person more capable.

The future should have more makers, not fewer.

If there’s something you wish existed, make it. We’ll build the tools to help you.

Mauricio Gomes, founder
